My Personal Philosophy on Vibe Coding

I don’t fully vibe code anything I TRULY care about.

The source of this philosophy comes from the article, Why superintelligence != superhuman.

AI knows a million more libraries, coding languages, and techniques than I can even name off the top of my head. But right now, I am still the master of my own destiny.

I trust it with implementing what I consider “boilerplate” — what has already been tried-and-done, the tedious processes I can describe much faster than I can build myself. I trust it as a partner in planning, to offer me viewpoints I might not have considered before.

When doing the things I do care about, I make sure to understand each line, each component, and understand WHY it’s done that way.

I don’t trust it to make decisions for me. I don’t trust it to perfectly obey my instructions. I don’t trust it to live my life for me.

I believe in this age of automation, the most valuable is whatever’s authentically YOURS. I never let AI edit any writing I care about. I never let it have the final say in any decision I make. Because whatever it is — it might be messy, it might be ill-informed, it might not be optimal — but it’s mine, well and truly mine. And if I make the wrong choice, then I’ll be damned, but keep in mind…

LLMs right now are still fundamentally pattern-matching machines. I’m a person, and I will always learn, remember, and grow better than any piece of software or automation anywhere.
